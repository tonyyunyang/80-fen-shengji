# Community guidelines · 社区约定

Eighty is a place to enjoy a game and build it together. Be welcoming, patient and constructive. Discuss code, rules and design without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and sharing another person's private information are not welcome.

维护者会酌情处理不友善的行为，包括编辑或删除不当内容、关闭讨论或限制参与。请尊重不同背景与熟练程度的玩家和贡献者；对事不对人，不骚扰、不歧视、不公开他人隐私。

For a moderation concern, open a brief issue without personal details and ask the maintainer how to continue privately. Do not publish another person's private information. Software vulnerabilities have a separate [security reporting policy](SECURITY.md).

This is a small project maintained in spare time. Moderation and review may not be immediate.
